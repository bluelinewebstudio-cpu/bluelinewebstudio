Since 1978

Founded in a Downers Grove home, built into a Chicagoland institution

Founded by Bob Carlson in 1978 out of his home in Downers Grove, IL, Pro Electric began as a small start-up electrical contracting company. It has since grown into a highly reputable business providing the Chicagoland area with reliable electrical services for over 45 years.

In the late 1980s, Pro Electric was among the first local companies to enter the residential generator market, giving customers a reliable, automated solution to power failures. With decades of experience installing and maintaining generator systems, we've developed into one of the premier Chicagoland full-service dealers for quality back-up power.
