Standby Generators

With growing demand for electricity and an aging power grid, a standby generator is a valuable asset for any home or business. When the power goes out, your system switches on automatically to cover essential circuits — so you stay safe, whether you're home or away.

  • Residential & industrial units, 8kW to 500kW gensets
  • Natural gas & diesel-powered systems
  • Comprehensive system design, installation & maintenance
  • Factory-trained & certified installation staff
  • Authorized Kohler, Generac & Winco dealer, service & warranty provider

We also service Cummins-Onan, Briggs & Stratton, Eaton, Honda and other generator manufacturers.
